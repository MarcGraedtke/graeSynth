In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and has no forms. It stores nothing in your browser.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Railway Corporation, San Francisco, USA (railway.com). The servers are located in the EU West region (Netherlands). Railway processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

With every request your browser transmits technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested address, amount of data transferred, the previously visited page (referrer), and browser and operating system. This data is used only to deliver the site and keep it secure. It is not combined with other data. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the stable and secure operation of the website.

As Railway is a US company, access from the USA cannot be ruled out. The transfer is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).

3D viewer

Only when you click “Load 3D model” on a module page does your browser load the 3D viewer's program libraries from the content delivery network jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). The network operator receives your IP address and your browser's technical data. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (your click); without the click no connection is made.

Contact by email

If you email us, we use your details only to answer your enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them when they are no longer needed for this and no statutory retention period applies.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Just write to the email address above.
